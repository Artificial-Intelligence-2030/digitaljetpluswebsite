Artem: from launch to 15K monthly visitors

We built Artem a complete e-commerce platform, then grew sales 250% and traffic past 15K visitors a month.

E-Commerce E-commerce development Payment integration Inventory system Digital marketing campaign
ClientArtem
IndustryE-commerce / retail
ServicesE-commerce development, payments, inventory, campaigns
Headline result+250% sales

The challenge

Artem wanted to sell its retail range online. Like many retailers making the jump to e-commerce, it faced a familiar mix: no unified storefront, stock tracked by hand, and a checkout that was not built to turn paid traffic into completed orders.

What we did

We designed and built the store end to end, connected the systems an online shop runs on, and launched campaigns to bring qualified buyers to it.

  • E-commerce development: storefront, product pages, and a custom cart
  • Payment integration wired directly into checkout
  • An inventory system that keeps stock levels accurate across the catalog
  • A digital marketing campaign to drive and convert traffic

The results

Sales increased 250%, monthly visitors climbed past 15K, and shoppers rate the store 4.8.
