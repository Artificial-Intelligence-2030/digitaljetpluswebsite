TechStart: 320% revenue growth in one year

A 12-month growth program took TechStart Inc. into 3 new markets, added 15 partnerships, and grew revenue 320%.

Business Development 12-month growth program Market expansion Partnership development Sales optimization
ClientTechStart Inc.
IndustryTechnology / B2B services
ServicesGrowth program, expansion, partnerships, sales
Headline result+320% revenue growth

The challenge

TechStart Inc. wanted to grow beyond the market it already served. A challenge we often see with B2B technology firms: one market carrying all the revenue, partnerships pursued ad hoc, and a sales process that depends on a few people's personal networks.

What we did

We ran a 12-month growth program that worked the problem from three sides: which markets to enter, which partners to pursue, and how the sales engine should run.

  • A structured 12-month growth program
  • Market expansion planning and entry into 3 new markets
  • Partnership development that produced 15 new partnerships
  • Sales optimization to shorten the path from lead to contract

The results

Revenue grew 320% in one year, TechStart entered 3 new markets, and 15 new partnerships are now in place.
