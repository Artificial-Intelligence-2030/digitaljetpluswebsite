FoodHub: 150K followers and counting

Always-on management of Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok turned FoodHub's accounts into a channel with 150K+ new followers and 5M+ impressions.

Social Media Instagram, Facebook and TikTok management Content creation Community management
ClientFoodHub
IndustryRestaurants / hospitality
ServicesSocial management, content, community
Headline result+400% engagement

The challenge

FoodHub's restaurants had fans but no rhythm online. A challenge we often see in hospitality: posting happens when someone finds time, comments and messages go unanswered, and the food never gets photographed the way it deserves.

What we did

We took over day-to-day management of Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok, built a content engine around the food itself, and gave the community a team that actually responds.

  • Instagram, Facebook and TikTok management on a consistent schedule
  • Content creation built around food shoots and short-form video
  • Community management for comments, messages, and reviews

The results

Engagement rose 400%, the accounts gained more than 150K followers, and posts have now passed 5M impressions.
