Common questions

What can AI automation actually do for my business?+

It takes over work that repeats: answering the same questions on WhatsApp, booking appointments, extracting data from documents, generating weekly reports, and routing leads to the right person. If a task follows rules and happens often, it can usually be automated.

Do I need technical knowledge to use it?+

No. We build the automation, connect it to the tools you already use, and train your team on exactly what to watch. Support retainers cover monitoring and improvements so it keeps working as your business changes.

How is it priced?+

By scope. Most clients start with one chatbot or workflow from UGX 1,500,000, then expand once the first automation proves its value in saved hours. Ongoing monitoring starts at UGX 600,000 per month.
