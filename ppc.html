Common questions

Is the management fee separate from ad spend?+

Yes. Our fee covers strategy, setup, creative, and optimization. You pay Google, Meta, or other platforms directly for the advertising itself, so you always see exactly where the media budget goes.

What is the minimum budget to start?+

Management starts at UGX 500,000 per month, and a workable ad budget depends on your market, competition, and goals. In the discovery call we estimate the budget needed to reach your targets before you commit to anything.

How fast do PPC results show?+

Paid campaigns can drive traffic from the first day they run. The first weeks go to testing and tuning, so cost per result usually improves steadily. Conversion tracking is set up before launch so every result is measured.
