Common questions

Which platforms do you cover?+

We work across the major platforms your customers use, including Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, and X. In the discovery call we identify where your audience actually spends time and focus the plan there. You do not pay for platforms that will not perform.

How often will you post?+

Posting frequency depends on your tier and platforms, and it is fixed in your monthly scope. Every plan includes a content calendar so you can see what is coming. Consistency matters more than raw volume, and we plan for both.

Do I approve content before it goes out?+

Yes. You get the content calendar for approval each month, and nothing publishes without your sign-off. Once we know your voice, many clients let us publish directly from the approved calendar to move faster. You can cancel anytime.
