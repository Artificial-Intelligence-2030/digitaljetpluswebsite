Digital Jetplus is a full-service digital marketing agency based in Kampala, Uganda. We help companies across East Africa turn marketing budgets into measurable business results.

Strategy first. Craft always. Egos never.

We started Digital Jetplus with a simple frustration: too many businesses were paying for marketing activity and getting nothing they could point to on the balance sheet.

So we built the agency we wanted to hire. Every campaign gets a target, every channel gets a number, and every report answers one question: what did this actually earn you?

How we think

Four values that decide how we work when nobody is watching.

Strategy before pixels

We do not start designing, writing, or spending until we know exactly what result the work is supposed to produce.

Craft is the strategy

Median work gets median results. We sweat the details because quality is what makes campaigns convert.

Data over opinions

Debates end at the dashboard. We test, measure, and let the numbers decide what ships next.

Partners, not vendors

We win when you win. Most of our clients have been with us for years because we treat their budgets like our own money.

How an engagement runs

The same disciplined loop, whether it is a landing page or a 12-month growth program.

01

Discover

We audit your current presence, market, and numbers. You get a clear picture of what is working and what is leaking.

02

Strategize

We define targets, channels, budgets, and a timeline you sign off on. No vague deliverables.

03

Build & launch

Our specialists produce and ship: campaigns, websites, content, apps. You review milestones, not surprises.

04

Scale

We report transparently, double down on what performs, and cut what does not. Then we raise the bar.
