Basile: 5.2:1 return on ad spend

Structured Google and Meta campaigns turned Basile's ad budget into $180K of tracked revenue at a 5.2:1 ROAS.

PPC Google Ads Meta ads Conversion tracking Weekly optimization
ClientBasile
IndustryConsumer goods
ServicesGoogle Ads, Meta ads, tracking, optimization
Headline result5.2:1 ROAS

The challenge

Basile was investing in ads without a clear picture of what those clicks were worth. A challenge we often see with paid media: campaigns launch fast, tracking arrives late, and budget keeps flowing to ads that never convert.

What we did

We rebuilt the account around measurable outcomes, putting clean conversion tracking in place first and only then scaling the campaigns worth scaling.

  • Google Ads campaigns built around high-intent searches
  • Meta ads to reach and re-engage likely buyers
  • Conversion tracking tied to sales, not just clicks
  • Weekly optimization to cut wasted spend and scale winners

The results

The account now returns 5.2:1 on ad spend, has generated $180K in tracked revenue, and runs at a 42% lower cost per acquisition.
