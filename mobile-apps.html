Common questions

Should I build native or cross-platform?+

Cross-platform development reaches iOS and Android from one codebase and keeps costs lower. Native makes sense when you need deep device features or maximum performance. We recommend the approach that fits your features and budget, and the proposal states it clearly.

How long does an app take to build?+

A simple app is scoped from UGX 6,000,000, while medium and complex apps scale with their features. The timeline is stated in the proposal and tied to milestones, so you can see progress at every stage. Store submission and launch are part of the scope.

Do you handle app store approval and updates?+

Yes. App store submission is included for both the Apple App Store and Google Play, and we handle the review process. Post-launch maintenance keeps the app current with updates after release.
