LearnHub: a platform built for 25K students

We built LearnHub an end-to-end learning platform that now serves 25K+ active students across 500+ courses.

EdTech Platform development Course management system Video streaming Quizzes and certification
ClientLearnHub
IndustryEducation technology
ServicesPlatform, course system, streaming, certification
Headline result25K+ active students

The challenge

LearnHub was running courses across disconnected tools. A challenge we often see in education: lessons hosted in one place, quizzes tracked in another, and no single home where students and instructors can actually work.

What we did

We designed and built one platform that carries the full learning journey, from enrollment to certificate.

  • Platform development for students and instructors
  • A course management system for organizing and publishing classes
  • Video streaming built into every course
  • Quizzes and certification tied to course completion

The results

The platform now carries 25K+ active students across 500+ courses, with completion holding steady at 98%.
