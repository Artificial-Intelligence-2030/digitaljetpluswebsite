Common questions

Do the monthly packages require a long-term contract?+

No. Monthly packages run month to month. Annual packages exist if you want the 20% discount, but nothing is locked in by default, and you can pay per quarter instead if that fits your cash flow.

Can I really afford this as an individual or small business?+

That is exactly who these tiers were built for. A Starter social package costs less than most people spend on airtime and data in a month, and our entry website costs about the same as a mid-range laptop. Tell us your budget and we will shape the smallest package that can genuinely work.

What does a web development project include?+

Every project includes discovery, design, development, content entry, SEO basics, launch, and training on the admin tools. E-commerce projects additionally include payment integration for mobile money and cards, and inventory setup.

Are ad spend budgets included in the PPC fee?+

No. Our fee covers strategy, setup, creative, and management. Your ad budget is paid directly to the platforms so you keep full visibility and control of it. For meaningful results we recommend at least UGX 500,000 per month in ad spend on top of the management fee.

How do I pay?+

Mobile money, bank transfer, or cash for Kampala clients. International clients receive a USD invoice. Payment schedules are agreed in the proposal, and smaller projects can be split into milestones.
