Common questions

How fast is the turnaround?+

Simple social videos are delivered faster than full brand productions because there is less to shoot and edit. The exact schedule is stated in your quote before we start, and revision rounds are included in that timeline.

Do you handle filming and locations?+

Yes. We handle concept, scripting, filming, and direction, working on location or at your premises. The final quote depends on length, locations, and the level of motion graphics, so you know the full cost upfront.

What formats do I receive?+

You receive the main edit plus subtitles and platform cutdowns in every format you need, sized for each channel you publish on. The final cut is one you approved through the included revision rounds.
