Common questions

What does a session look like?+

A consultation is a focused 4-hour working session with your strategist. We come prepared with market and competitor analysis, and you leave with specific decisions and action steps. It is working time, not a lecture.

Do you work remotely or in person?+

Both. We are based in Kampala and can meet in person, and we run remote sessions for clients anywhere. The monthly retainer includes two sessions plus ongoing support between them.

What deliverables do I get?+

Every engagement produces written outputs: growth analysis, positioning, funnel or operations plans, and prioritized actions. Project-based work is scoped and priced separately. You always know what you are paying for before we start.
